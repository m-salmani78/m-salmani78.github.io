About Me

I am an M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Tehran, working at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and human preference alignment. My research focuses on instruction following, theory of mind in LLMs, and building stable RL frameworks for multi-constraint tasks.

I have industry experience as an NLP Engineer at Hamrahe Aval (MCI), where I developed and deployed resource-efficient LLM pipelines, and I recently completed a research internship at ESSEC Business School in France on dynamic curriculum learning. I enjoy bridging rigorous research with practical engineering — from fine-tuning and RLHF to scalable inference with vLLM.

Feel free to reach out if you find my work interesting or would like to collaborate.
