About Me

I am an M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Tehran, working at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and human preference alignment. My research focuses on instruction following, theory of mind in LLMs, and building stable RL frameworks for multi-constraint tasks.

I have industry experience as an NLP Engineer at MCI (Hamrahe Aval), where I developed and deployed resource-efficient LLM pipelines using supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. I am currently a research intern at ESSEC Business School in France on dynamic curriculum learning, and a research assistant in the NLP Lab at University of Tehran, working on human preference alignment and role-playing in LLMs.

Feel free to reach out if you find my work interesting or would like to collaborate.

ACL 2026 Main Conference
